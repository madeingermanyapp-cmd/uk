## Admin controls

- **Settings > Media**: choose a named homepage section, gallery, venue or CMS page; upload, reuse, replace, remove and reorder images. Review source/usage information before publication.
- **Settings > Website content**: edit the grounds/gallery headings, descriptions, homepage text, navigation and section visibility; preview and publish drafts.
- **Settings > Pages & legal content**: edit page text, titles, SEO and publication status. A draft never overwrites the live version until explicitly published.

Only published pages appear automatically in the footer. Unpublished drafts are inaccessible to unauthenticated visitors. A client's instruction to use supplied photos is recorded as provenance without asserting independent verification of third-party copyright ownership.
